Research and projects

Causal inference

  • Synthetic Control Method in Python

    What would West Germany's economy have looked like without reunification? A Python walkthrough of the synthetic control method that builds that counterfactual from comparable countries and measures the gap after 1990.

    Project page

  • Causal Discovery

    Can you recover cause and effect from observational data alone? A survey of causal search methods, from constraint-based and score-based algorithms to LiNGAM.

    PDF report

    Notebooks that treat causal questions directly in probability theory, using do-calculus and structural causal models.

Writing

Articles where I take a method and point it at a question I actually wanted answered.

  • Optimizing Ambulance Response Time Using Uber Movement Data

    Towards Data Science, August 2020

    I turned Uber Movement travel times across about 1,000 London zones into a network, then ran Monte Carlo simulations to see how dispatch strategies and the 2016 Tower Bridge closure changed ambulance response times.

    Cited in a UC Berkeley study that simulates police response times in Chicago.

  • Predicting Song Skipping on Spotify

    Towards Data Science, January 2021

    A gradient-boosted classifier, tuned with Bayesian optimization, learns to predict my skips from audio features alone, trained on a full year of my own Spotify listening history. It was right 74% of the time.

  • Finding Your Next Beloved Artists

    Towards Data Science, March 2021

    I mapped the artists connected to my top 50 on Spotify as a network, then ranked the ones I had never heard of by how closely they tie to my favorites.

  • Algorithmic Trading using Sentiment Analysis on r/WallStreetBets

    Medium

    I scraped r/wallstreetbets after the 2021 GameStop short squeeze, scored the sentiment around its ten most-mentioned tickers, and tested whether the crowd's mood one hour predicts the stock's move the next.
